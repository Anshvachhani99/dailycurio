A protein-rich breakfast keeps you full longer and helps you avoid mid-morning snacking. These five ideas are vegetarian, simple, and fast.

## 1. Paneer bhurji toast

Crumble 100g of paneer and sauté with onion, tomato, turmeric, and green chilli. Spread on whole-wheat toast. **About 18–20g protein.**

## 2. Moong dal chilla

Blend soaked moong dal with ginger, chilli, and salt into a batter. Cook thin pancakes on a hot pan. Make the batter the night before to save time. **About 12–15g protein for two chillas.**

## 3. Greek yogurt power bowl

Top a cup of thick Greek yogurt (or hung curd) with nuts, seeds, and fruit. Add a spoon of peanut butter for extra protein. **About 15–20g protein.**

## 4. Besan and veggie omelette-style pancake

Mix gram flour (besan) with water, chopped vegetables, and spices. Cook like an omelette. **About 10–12g protein.**

## 5. Peanut butter banana smoothie

Blend milk (or soy milk), a banana, a spoon of peanut butter, and a few oats. Great when you're rushing out the door. **About 12–15g protein.**

## Make it even easier

- **Prep on Sunday.** Soak dal, chop vegetables, and portion nuts for the week.
- **Keep staples stocked:** paneer, curd, besan, peanut butter, oats.
- **Pair protein with fibre** — vegetables, fruit, or whole grains — for steady energy.

Protein numbers are approximate and depend on brands and portion sizes.
