AI chat assistants are now part of student life. The trick is using them to **learn faster**, not to skip learning altogether.

## 1. Ask for explanations at your level

Try: *"Explain photosynthesis like I'm in Class 8, then give me a harder version."* You can keep asking follow-up questions until it clicks.

## 2. Generate practice questions

Paste in your notes and ask for 10 multiple-choice questions with answers hidden at the end. Self-testing is one of the most effective study methods.

## 3. Make flashcards automatically

Ask the AI to turn a chapter summary into question-and-answer pairs, then import them into a free flashcard app for spaced repetition.

## 4. Get feedback on your writing

Instead of asking AI to write your essay, write it yourself and ask: *"What are the three weakest points in this argument?"* You'll improve much faster.

## 5. Build a study timetable

Tell it your exam dates, subjects, and free hours each day. Ask for a realistic plan with revision days built in.

## 6. Summarise long readings

Use it to get an overview before you read in detail — not instead of reading.

## Rules to stay honest (and smart)

- **Always check facts.** AI can be confidently wrong, especially with numbers and dates.
- **Follow your school's policy** on AI use for assignments.
- **Don't paste private information** like ID numbers or passwords.
- **Do the thinking yourself.** Use AI as a coach, not a ghostwriter.

The students who benefit most from AI are the ones who use it to ask better questions.
