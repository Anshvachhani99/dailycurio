Battery anxiety is real. Before you buy a power bank, try these settings — they work on most Android phones and iPhones.

## 1. Turn on dark mode (especially on OLED screens)

On OLED displays, black pixels are switched off completely. Dark mode can cut screen power use significantly when you're using apps with dark backgrounds.

## 2. Lower the screen refresh rate

120Hz looks smooth, but it uses more power. Switching to 60Hz — or "adaptive" — is one of the biggest single savings you can make.

## 3. Restrict background app refresh

Social media and shopping apps constantly refresh in the background. On iPhone, go to **Settings → General → Background App Refresh**. On Android, look for **Battery → Background usage limits**.

## 4. Limit location access

Many apps ask for "Always" location access when they only need it "While using the app." Review this list once a month.

## 5. Shorten the screen timeout

Set your screen to turn off after 30 seconds instead of 2 minutes. It sounds small, but you unlock your phone dozens of times a day.

## 6. Switch off always-on display

It's convenient, but it's also a screen that never truly sleeps. Turn it off or schedule it for daytime only.

## 7. Use Wi-Fi instead of mobile data when you can

Searching for a weak mobile signal drains the battery quickly. At home or the office, Wi-Fi is far more efficient.

## Bonus: protect long-term battery health

- Avoid charging to 100% every night; many phones now have an "optimized charging" option.
- Keep your phone out of direct sunlight and hot cars.
- Use a good-quality charger and cable.

Try a few of these today and compare your screen-on time tomorrow. Most people see a noticeable difference.
