Everyone knows Goa, Manali, and Shimla. But on a long weekend, those places can feel more like a traffic jam than a getaway. The good news: India is full of smaller destinations that are cheaper, calmer, and often more beautiful.

## 1. Chikmagalur, Karnataka

Coffee estates, misty viewpoints, and easy treks like Mullayanagiri make this a perfect two-day trip from Bengaluru. Homestays inside plantations are affordable and usually include home-cooked meals.

## 2. Gokarna, Karnataka

Think of it as Goa's quieter cousin. Om Beach, Kudle Beach, and Half Moon Beach are connected by coastal walking trails, and beach shacks are budget-friendly.

## 3. Lansdowne, Uttarakhand

A peaceful cantonment town about six hours from Delhi. There are no big malls or nightlife — just pine forests, old churches, and clean air.

## 4. Ziro, Arunachal Pradesh

Rice fields surrounded by pine-covered hills, plus the warm Apatani tribal culture. It takes planning (you need an Inner Line Permit), but it's unforgettable.

## 5. Bundi, Rajasthan

Stepwells, painted palaces, and blue lanes — without the crowds of Jaipur or Udaipur. Guesthouses here are some of the best value in Rajasthan.

## 6. Majuli, Assam

The world's largest river island is famous for its monasteries (satras), mask-making, and slow village life. Take the ferry from Jorhat.

## 7. Tarkarli, Maharashtra

Clear water, scuba diving at affordable prices, and fresh Malvani seafood. Ideal for a quick escape from Mumbai or Pune.

## 8. Kasol's quieter neighbour: Tosh, Himachal Pradesh

Kasol is busy now, but a short drive further takes you to Tosh, with snow peaks, waterfalls, and simple cafés.

## 9. Hampi, Karnataka

Boulder landscapes and the ruins of the Vijayanagara empire. Rent a bicycle and you can explore most of it for very little.

## 10. Varkala, Kerala

Red cliffs above a long beach, yoga studios, and a relaxed crowd. Off-season (June to September) prices drop sharply.

## Quick budget tips

- **Travel by overnight train or bus** to save one night of accommodation.
- **Book homestays** instead of hotels — they are cheaper and the food is better.
- **Go in the shoulder season**, just before or after peak months.
- **Carry cash** for smaller towns where UPI coverage can be patchy.

A weekend doesn't need to be expensive to feel like a real break. Pick one place from this list, pack light, and go.
